2. Service Overview

Harmony Timer is a fitness and wellness timer application designed to assist users with exercise, stretching, meditation, and related activities. The App provides various timing modes and features to help you structure your workouts and mindfulness sessions. No user account is required to use Harmony Timer, and the App does not collect personal information from you (all data is stored locally on your device, as detailed in Section 5).

Key Features of Harmony Timer include:

  • Training Mode: Create structured workout timers with segments for warm-up, exercise, and cool-down phases to guide your training sessions.
  • Rounds Mode: Configure interval training timers by setting a round duration, rest/break duration, and the number of rounds, ideal for boxing, high-intensity interval training (HIIT), or circuit workouts.
  • Stretching Mode: Time your stretching routines with options for a simple continuous stretch timer or sectionalized timers targeting specific areas (upper body, lower body, or core), helping ensure balanced flexibility training.
  • Meditation Mode: Facilitate mindfulness practice with either self-guided meditation timers or guided sessions that include preset or customizable breathing patterns, aiding in relaxation and breath control.
  • Progress Tracking: View your activity history on a calendar interface that logs completed sessions. This progress tracker allows you to monitor your consistency and track which days you have completed training, stretching, or meditation activities.
  • Customization and Settings: Adjust various App settings to enhance your experience. You can toggle background music on or off during sessions, erase or reset your locally stored data (e.g., clear your activity logs and settings through the data deletion option), and access external links to resources or community pages.
