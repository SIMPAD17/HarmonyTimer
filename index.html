Questions

Good to know.

Does the bell ring with the phone locked?

Yes. When the phone is locked, each bell arrives as a time-sensitive notification with the bell's sound, and the Live Activity shows the round and the time left. Notification sounds follow the silent switch, so with Silent on, turn on Background Music in Settings to hear every bell.

Which martial arts is it for?

Any of them. Rounds works for striking and grappling alike, Train covers conditioning and drills, Stretch covers recovery and Meditate covers focus. Quick start has ready-made formats for Boxing, Kickboxing, Muay Thai, MMA, BJJ rolls and Tabata.

Do I need an account? Where is my data?

No account. Your training history stays on your device. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Does it work on iPad and iPhone Duo?

Yes. On iPad the setup screens use two columns. On iPhone Duo it adapts to every pose: folded, unfolded and stood on its side like a ring clock.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your Apple Account settings on the iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.
