Harmony Timer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Summary

  • Your workout history, presets, and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad. The app never uploads them, and we do not receive them.
  • The app has no account and no ads, and it does not track you.
  • Bells and the Live Activity are created on your device. The app sends no push notifications.
  • An image you share after a session is made on your device and goes only where you send it.
  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your name, email address, or payment details from a purchase.
  • To check Harmony Timer Pro, the app uses a subscription service provider. It receives an anonymous ID, a device identifier, and your purchase history, but not your name or contact details (section 3).
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and what you send.

This policy explains what information the Harmony Timer app for iPhone and iPad collects, why, and what choices you have. Harmony Timer is made by Omnis Dragon LLC, doing business as Journey On ("we", "us"), 116 Agnes Rd, Ste 200, Knoxville, Tennessee 37919-6306, United States. We are responsible for the information described here; in European and UK law we are the "controller". Questions: support@journeyon.co. This policy replaces the policy of April 11, 2025.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • Your history, presets, and settings (sessions you completed, saved timers, custom breathing patterns, sound settings, and which belts and streak milestones the app has shown you) are saved in the app's storage on your device. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, they are included in that backup, under Apple's or your computer's terms. Deleting the app deletes them from the device. We do not receive them.
  • Timers run on your device. Your belt and streak are worked out on the device from your history. Nothing about your sessions is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Images you share. When you tap Share after a session, the app draws an image of it on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The image goes only to the app or person you choose; we do not receive it.
  • No other access. The app does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts, your photos, or Apple Health.

2. Notifications and Live Activities

  • Bells when the phone is locked. When you start a timer, the app schedules local notifications on your device for the coming phase changes, so the bell rings while the phone is locked. They are time-sensitive, so they can ring during a Focus. They are created and delivered by iOS on your device; no server is involved, and the app does not send push notifications.
  • Live Activity. While a timer runs, the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island show its phase and the time left. iOS shows this on your device only.
  • Your choice. iOS asks for your permission before the first notification. You can change notifications, time-sensitive notifications, and Live Activities at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Harmony Timer. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can see a running timer's phase.

3. Harmony Timer Pro and the subscription check

Purchases go through Apple. Apple processes payments and manages your Apple Account under Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Apple does not give us your name, email address, or payment details.

To check Harmony Timer Pro, the app uses a subscription service provider. The app makes this check when it starts and is online, whether or not you subscribe. The provider receives:

  • An anonymous ID that the app creates. It contains nothing about you.
  • A device identifier: the random ID that iOS gives to our apps on your device.
  • Your Harmony Timer purchase history from the App Store, such as the plan, the dates, and the price.
  • The technical details that come with any internet request, such as the iOS version, the app version, the country of your App Store, and the IP address.

We use this information only to provide Harmony Timer Pro, to prevent fraud, and to count trials and subscriptions. It is not linked to your identity, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. The provider processes it only for us and protects it as this policy does. It is kept as a purchase record, stored in the United States. To have it deleted, see section 11.

4. Support emails

Contact Support in the app's Settings opens a new email in your Mail app. When you email us, we receive your email address, any name your email includes, and what you send. We use it only to answer you. We keep support emails for 24 months after the last message, then delete them. Our email is hosted by Google (Google Workspace).

5. Information Apple may give us

If you allow it in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements > Share With App Developers), Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics for the app. App Store Connect also gives us totals such as downloads and sales. Apple provides these under its own privacy policy, and they do not identify you to us.

6. Links to other sites and apps

The app links to our website, our Instagram page, and our Etsy shop. They open outside the app, and the privacy policies of Instagram and Etsy apply there.

7. Our website

The pages at harmonytimer.journeyon.co are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log the IP address of visitors for security. The pages load a font from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when you open them. The pages use no cookies and no analytics.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as California law defines these terms.
  • We do not show ads, use the advertising identifier, work with data brokers, or track you as Apple defines tracking.
  • We do not use analytics tools that record how you use the app.

9. Legal bases (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

  • The subscription check: to perform our contract with you (providing Harmony Timer Pro), and our legitimate interests in offering the free features alongside Pro, preventing fraud, and knowing how many trials and subscriptions we have.
  • Support emails: to answer your request.
  • Notifications and Live Activities: your permission in iOS. They are processed only on your device.

10. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it or delete the app. Copies in device backups stay until those backups are deleted.
  • Purchase record (section 3): as long as we offer Harmony Timer Pro, unless you ask us to delete it sooner (section 11).
  • Support emails: as in section 4.
  • Apple's records: under Apple's privacy policy.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your history. In the app, Settings > Reset Progress Data deletes your session history. Deleting the app deletes everything stored in it.
  • Notifications and Live Activities. Turn them off at any time in the iOS Settings app. The timers still run and play their sounds while the app is open.
  • Delete your purchase record. Email support@journeyon.co with the order ID from Apple's receipt email for your Harmony Timer Pro purchase. We will have the record deleted within 30 days and confirm by email. Deleting the record does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. If you never bought Harmony Timer Pro, your record holds no purchases and we cannot tell which one is yours; deleting the app stops it from being updated.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, you can also complain to your data protection authority. Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws have similar rights, and we will not treat you differently for using them. To use a right, email support@journeyon.co. We answer within the time the law sets, and we will not ask for more information than we need.

12. Children

Harmony Timer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only information that leaves the device without the user sending it is the subscription check in section 3, which contains no name or contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Information on your device is protected by iOS. The subscription check uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No system is completely secure.

14. International users

We are based in the United States, and the purchase record is stored in the United States. Transfers from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are made under standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for a copy at support@journeyon.co.

15. Changes to this policy

We post changes on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change affects what the app collects, we say so in the app's update notes before it takes effect, and we update the App Store privacy information.
