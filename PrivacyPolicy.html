Privacy Policy

Introduction

Last Updated: April 11, 2025

Harmony Timer (the "App") is provided by Omnis Dragon LLC dba Journey On ("Company," "we," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how the App operates and handles your data. In summary, we do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. The App is designed to function entirely offline, ensuring that your data remains on your device.

By using Harmony Timer, you agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection

We value your privacy and have intentionally built Harmony Timer to collect no personal data. You are not required to create an account or provide any personal details to use the App. We do not gather any of the following information:

  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or any other personally identifying information.
  • Usage Data: We do not track how you use the App, and we do not collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • Device Data: We do not access or collect device identifiers, contact lists, photos, or any other information from your device.
  • Sensitive Personal Data: We do not collect any health data, location data, financial information, or any sensitive information about you.

The only data the App handles is the information you input yourself to use its features (for example, your custom workout timers, stretching routines, meditation sessions, or timer presets). This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to Journey On or any third party. In other words, Harmony Timer operates without sending any data to external servers.

Use of Data

Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not use your data for any profiling, advertising, or other purposes. All data that you enter into Harmony Timer (such as setting up a timer or routine) is used only within the App to provide you with the intended functionality (e.g., to run your timers, log sessions, or play sounds for your workouts).

This information is not sent outside your device. We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with any third parties. In short, your data stays with you, and is used solely to enable the App's features for your personal use.

Data Storage and Security

All user data is stored locally on your device. Harmony Timer saves your workout timers, routines, meditation sessions, presets, and any other preferences directly on your device. No data is ever uploaded to our servers, ensuring that your information remains private to you.

Because your data never leaves your device, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend that you protect your device with a passcode, biometric lock, or other security measures to prevent unauthorized access to your apps and data.

If you lose access to your device or it is damaged, we do not have any copy of your Harmony Timer data to restore. Similarly, if you uninstall the App or clear its data, the information you have stored (such as your saved timers and routines) will be deleted permanently. We cannot recover data that was only stored on your device.

Permissions and Third-Party Access

Harmony Timer does not request any special permissions that access your personal information or sensors beyond what is required to provide its basic functionality. Specifically:

  • Camera: Not used. The App does not use your camera.
  • Microphone: Not used. The App does not record audio or access your microphone.
  • Location: Not used. We do not request or track your location.
  • Push Notifications: Not used. The App does not send push notifications, so it does not request notification permissions.

The App uses a few standard device features—such as device vibration for haptic feedback and sound playback for workout cues—to enhance your experience. These features operate solely on your device and do not result in your data being transmitted elsewhere.

We do not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash reporting tools. This means no third-party services are used to collect data from your device. No third party has access to any of your data through our App.

External Links

Harmony Timer may contain links that direct you to external websites or services (for example, our social media pages or our shop on Etsy). When you tap on these links, your device's web browser or appropriate app will open and navigate to the external site. Please note that once you leave Harmony Timer, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. Those external sites are not operated by us, and they have their own privacy policies and data practices.

We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external website or service you visit via links from Harmony Timer. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content or privacy practices of any third-party sites or services.

Children's Privacy

Harmony Timer is not specifically targeted towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the App does not require account sign-up or personal details, children can use the App's basic functionality without providing personal data. However, if you are under 13, please use this App only with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian.

If we discover that a child under 13 has inadvertently provided us with personal information (for example, by contacting us via email), we will delete that information immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and have any concerns about your child's use of Harmony Timer, please contact us so we can address the issue promptly.

User Rights

Because we do not collect or store any of your personal data on our servers, you maintain full control over your information. Privacy laws (such as the GDPR or CCPA) generally give users rights to access, correct, or delete their personal data. In the context of Harmony Timer, no personal data is held by Journey On, so there is no information for us to provide or erase on your behalf.

All data you generate in the App (for example, your saved timers or session logs) remains on your device. You have the right to delete this data at any time by deleting the App or clearing its data from your device storage. Since we never receive your data, there is no need to request data deletion from us — you already have direct control.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality, legal requirements, or other operational reasons. If we make any material changes, we will post the updated Privacy Policy within the App (in the Privacy Policy section) and update the "Last Updated" date at the top.

Your continued use of Harmony Timer after any changes to this Privacy Policy have been posted constitutes your acceptance of those changes. If you do not agree with an updated policy, you should discontinue use of the App.
